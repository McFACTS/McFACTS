# Contribution Guidelines

Before making any contribution, please read our [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and familiarize
yourself with our AI Policy below.

> [!NOTE]
> These are living guidelines, which you can expect to change. Please check back regularly
> and reach out if you need anything clarified.

The primary goal of the McFACTS code and the McFACTS collaboration is to produce tools and knowledge that 
deepen our understanding of Active Galactic Nuclei and further promote scientific exploration. As such, 
all changes and proposals should be made through a lens that supports and builds towards those ideals.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Before making any contributions, please join our mailing list and introduce yourself via the GitHub
> discussion forms or via email, which can be found on our [website](https://saavikford.wixsite.com/saavik/general-7).

> [!WARNING]
> Pull requests from external collaborators, especially individuals who have not historically worked 
> with the research topics and physics used in McFACTS, may have their pull request rejected if no
> prior communication with members of our collaboration exists.

## How to Contribute to McFACTS

A detailed guide on the steps to make a contribution can be found in our [contribution documentation](https://github.com/McFACTS/McFACTS/blob/main/docs/source/contribute.rst).

## AI Policy

Throughout this document "AI" shall generally refer to large language and generative models.

1. **The use of AI should not be the default.** If you don't use AI for McFACTS contributions, nothing else applies to you.
2. **Review the AIAL statement and flowchart before AI use.** If the use of AI is necessary in the scope of your contribution to McFACTS, please review the generative AI [statement and flowchart](https://aial.ie/blog/gen-ai-statement/) published by Trinity College Dublin's Artificial Intelligence Accountability Lab. 
3. **AI assistance must be documented for all contributions.** If applicable, please document the model, its version, and the prompt(s) used in the commit message and pull request description.
4. **Incidental AI use does not need documentation.** Using a search engine's AI summaries is sometimes unavoidable and generally does not need to be documented.
5. **Do not use AI to generate prose.** This is a blanket ban on the use of AI to generate prose. Some examples include, but are not limited to: documentation, paper contributions, commits and pull requests, text communication.

Suspected AI use and failure to comply with this policy may result in an outright rejection of any or all proposed contributions.

This policy is subject to change and may be updated without notice.
